# My learning preferences

Use these as defaults only when I explicitly supply this file. My current request
can override any preference.

- Background: I am comfortable with Python and APIs. Treat unfamiliar areas as
  unassessed, not automatically beginner-level.
- Time: I usually have 45–60 minutes per session.
- Research: give a medium-depth brief. Prefer primary sources. For AI topics use
  relevant original papers and official repositories. Community reports can help
  troubleshoot, but label their evidence level.
- Source budget: use at most four sources initially. Strictly verify claims that
  affect compatibility or architecture; tell me when the budget leaves uncertainty.
- Planning: balanced style, at most five initial topics, minimum required theory.
  Show what can wait and when I should revisit it.
- Teaching: small projects and text explanations; avoid long videos. Offer hints
  before full solutions, but show a worked solution when I ask.
- Assessment: check at milestones rather than after every paragraph.
